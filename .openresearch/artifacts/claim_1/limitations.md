# Limitations and deviations

The formal equality is a population-objective identity. Finite empirical
averages from different paired and unpaired samples are estimators of its three
expectations and are not asserted to equal a paired-only empirical NLL.

The full-setting numerical audit uses the paper's Swiss-Roll architecture and
sample counts before training because the claim concerns the objective
construction, not benchmark performance. Training performance is tested by the
later Swiss-Roll and weather claims.
