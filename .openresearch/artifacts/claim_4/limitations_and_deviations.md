# Claim 4 limitations and deviations

The efficiency measurement is machine-specific CPU throughput and cannot
establish an asymptotic complexity bound. It directly tests the paper-scale
2D task, not high-dimensional scalability. The manual density checker shares
the trained tensors but does not call the library distribution evaluator.

Raw JSON and checker output will be copied from the formal `orx logs` evidence
into a descendant evidence commit after the successful branch is frozen.
